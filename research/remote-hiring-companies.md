## How to use this

- **The CSV (`remote-hiring-companies.csv`)** has all 97 with columns:
  company, remoteStatus, confidence, headcount, over500, remoteRoleCount,
  remoteRoleTitles, workModelMix (rem/hyb/ons/unk counts), vertical, exampleUrl,
  and the verified policy note. Filter `remoteStatus = Yes` for the shortlist, or
  sort by `remoteRoleCount` to find the most remote-heavy employers (Surge 5,
  EvenUp 6, Cresta 4, Tribe 4, Zapier 3, Turing 3, Sema4 3).
- **Best remote fits for your profile** (intersecting this list with the
  fit-ranking): Forerunner, Auctor, Forge/Poetic, Lorikeet, Tailor, Tribe AI,
  Siena AI, Sema4.ai, Onebrief — all ✅-verified remote and high-fit.
- **Caveats:** ◽ role-data classifications reflect the specific roles captured in
  the July 2026 sweep, not every open role; a company marked onsite may post a
  remote role later. Re-check the live board before deciding.
