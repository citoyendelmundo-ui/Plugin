## 1. Your 2–3 competitive target titles

Ranked by fit to your profile:

**① Forward-Deployed Product Manager / Deployment Strategist** *(primary)*
The sweet spot. These roles own the customer side of an AI deployment: discovery,
scoping, workflow design, pilot delivery, adoption, and feeding learnings back to
product. They reward exactly your stack — customer discovery, qualitative
research, program-ops, domain fluency, and writing — while staying **light on
coding**. Canonical titles: *Forward Deployed Product Manager, Deployment
Strategist, Forward Deployed Strategist, AI Solutions Lead/Architect (non-SWE),
Agent Strategy Manager.* This title family is also the **densest hiring pocket in
the sector right now** (98 of 228 collected roles).

**② Strategic Projects / Delivery / Deployments Lead** *(strong secondary)*
The Fleet "Deployments Generalist" family generalized: own end-to-end delivery of
customer data/agent programs. Titles: *Strategic Projects Lead, Implementation
Delivery / AI Operator, Engagement Manager, Program Manager (AI), Product
Operations, Operations Generalist.* Best fit for your program/ops background;
often the fastest to convert because AI experience is "helpful not required."

**③ Product Manager / Product Researcher — Applied/Agentic AI** *(core, competitive)*
Straight PM/product-research at AI-data/evals/agent companies. Titles: *Product
Manager (AI/Agents), Senior PM, Product Manager–Research, AI Product Manager,
Product Researcher.* The **Product Manager–Research** and **Embedded/Research PM**
variants (Decagon, Anthropic, Percepta) are near-exact matches to your
product-research + intelligence background.

> **A caution that shapes the search:** many "Forward Deployed **Engineer**" and
> "Agent Engineer" postings (Sierra, Hebbia, Decagon FDE, OpenAI FDE) are
> genuinely **coding-heavy**. I kept the strongest of these visible but flagged
> them; your leverage is in the PM/strategist/architect (non-SWE) variants of the
> same forward-deployed motion.

### What the geography tells you
- **The sector is ~69% in-person, and in-person clusters hard in the SF Bay Area
  (~36%).** Your stated willingness to be onsite in the Bay is a real advantage —
  it unlocks the largest single pool.
- **Fully-remote is a solid ~31%** and over-indexes in the **delivery/program**
  and **data-labeling** families (customer-facing delivery travels; the work
  doesn't require a lab). If you ever need remote, weight titles ② and the data
  long tail.
- **NYC is the clear #2 hub (~8–14%)** and concentrates the enterprise-vertical
  agent companies (Percepta, Hebbia, EvenUp, General Intuition). Worth relaxing
  the geo filter for the Percepta/enterprise-vertical roles specifically.
- **Forward-deployed roles skew most in-person** (only ~25% remote in the
  collected set) because they embed with customers; delivery/program roles are
  the most remote-friendly.

## 5. Method, caveats & how to use the CSV

- **Coverage:** 97 companies across agentic-transformation pure-plays, frontier
  labs' forward-deployed teams, RL-env/data leaders, expert-data/labeling,
  evals/observability, enterprise-AI consultancies (BCG X, McKinsey QuantumBlack,
  Deloitte), and vertical agent startups (Harvey, Hebbia, Abridge, EvenUp, …).
- **Not exhaustive & time-sensitive:** postings open/close weekly; "likely-live"
  means indexed in July 2026, not re-verified at read time. Verify each link.
- **Comp:** shown where posted; a few are market estimates (flagged). Blank = not
  disclosed.
- **The CSV (`ai-sector-job-openings.csv`)** has all 228 rows with columns:
  company, title, titleFamily, location, workModel, primaryCity, seniority, comp,
  confidence, fitNote, url — sort/filter it to build your own shortlist (e.g.
  filter workModel=remote, or primaryCity=New York for the Percepta/vertical set
  excluded from the curated table by geo default).
- **Dedup:** rows deduped by company+title; a few near-duplicate reposts (e.g.
  Cresta EMEA variants) remain intentionally as distinct postings.
