# Force-Ranked Reading List — Long-Tail AI Companies (< 500 employees)

> From the 97 companies in the job inventory, I removed anything clearly >500 or
> already covered earlier in this project (Fleet, Surge, Mercor, Snorkel, Decagon,
> Sierra, Harvey, the frontier labs, etc. — you've read those), leaving **40
> genuinely long-tail names**. Each was enriched (real headcount, funding,
> traction) and scored 1–5 on four axes tuned to your profile — **role fit**
> (non-SWE PM/deployment/research), **profile leverage** (does the work reward
> customer discovery + qualitative research + domain writing + ops),
> **coding-lightness**, **traction/health** — plus an obscurity bonus. **5 were
> cut for exceeding 500 employees**, leaving **35 ranked below**. Run 2026-07-02.
>
> **Notability key:** ⚫ obscure · ◐ somewhat-known · ○ known (you may have seen it).

## Start here — the hidden gems (obscure + high-fit)

If you only read about five, read these. Each is small, under-the-radar, and the
open role is squarely non-SWE customer-discovery/deployment/research work:

1. **Amigo** (healthcare clinical agents, ~35, NYC) — The *AI Deployment
   Strategist* is explicitly the **non-engineering half of a two-person deployment
   pair** (paired with an agent engineer): map clinical workflows, own
   safety/quality framing, run delivery. Almost a job description of your profile.
   *Watch-out: NYC-onsite.*
2. **Forerunner** (GovTech / flood-risk geospatial, ~80, NYC, **remote**) —
   Remote *Deployment Strategist* rolling software out to local governments:
   B2G customer discovery, stakeholder synthesis, delivery. Mission-driven, and
   remote clears your geo bar cleanly.
3. **Auctor** (AI for enterprise-software *implementation*, ~25, NYC, remote) —
   Founded 2025, so the *Deployment Strategist* AND *Founding Product Operations
   Manager* are both open and both are pure discovery-workflow-delivery-ops. Their
   whole product automates the FDE/solutions-engineer job — you'd be shaping the
   function from the ground floor. *High ambiguity (very young/tiny).*
4. **Forge** (rebranded **Poetic**; enterprise process automation, ~30, SF) —
   *Forward Deployed Strategist* owning strategic enterprise accounts (adoption,
   expansion) under ambiguity. SF, so onsite works for you.
5. **Percepta** (General Catalyst's in-house applied-AI company, ~30, NYC) —
   The role that inspired this whole search. *Research Product Manager*, *Embedded
   PM*, *Embedded Researcher*, *Chief of Staff* — an unusually clean four-way match
   to product-research + intelligence + ops. *NYC, and a high-prestige bar.*

Runners-up worth the read: **Tailor** (composable ERP for AI agents, remote FDPM),
**Tribe AI** (AI-services *Delivery Lead* — pure program-ops, remote),
**FERMÀT** (AI-native D2C commerce — your D2C discovery background), **Lorikeet**
(AI support concierge, remote FDPM), **Siena AI** (autonomous CX, remote FDPM).

## How to read this list (and its limits)

- **Well-known ⚫→○ note:** a few high scorers are *not* obscure — **OpenEvidence**
  (#1 on raw fit, but a hyped clinical-AI name you've probably seen),
  **Fractional AI**, **Factory.ai**, **Profound**, **Redox**, **Peregrine**,
  **Crew AI**. They're kept because they're <500 and fit, but if "discover
  something new" is the goal, weight the ◐/⚫ rows.
- **The coding trap persists in the tail:** **Ascertain** and **Crew AI** rank
  low because their open roles are genuinely engineering-heavy despite
  forward-deployed titles — correctly demoted.
- **Geography:** many top gems are NYC (Amigo, Forerunner, Auctor, Percepta) or
  international (Tailor–Tokyo, Lorikeet–Sydney). Remote-friendly high-fit options:
  Forerunner, Tribe AI, Tailor, Lorikeet, Siena, Forge remote variants.
- **Headcount confidence varies** — several are estimates from Crunchbase/Tracxn/
  LinkedIn (flagged medium/low in the source data); boundary cases (Peregrine ~440,
  Onebrief ~345, Axion ~320) are comfortably under 500 but worth re-checking.
- **Full enriched data** (funding, investors, sources, all four sub-scores per
  company) is in `longtail-companies-enriched.csv`.
