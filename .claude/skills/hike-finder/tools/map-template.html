How this was made: elevation from AWS Terrain Tiles (USGS-derived, ~7 m cells), contoured every 20 ft. Lake-loop trails are placed by hand against the terrain and the park's trail layout, accurate to roughly 50–150 m. The Ice Age Trail pieces east of the East Bluff (Uplands, Roznos Meadow, Steinke Basin) are approximate corridors, shown dashed: their published lengths are 1.8 mi (Roznos Meadow) and 2.4 mi (Steinke Basin). Distances and climbing are computed from these drawn lines, so expect ±10–15%. Time uses your own pace: 3 mph on flat ground plus 1 hour per 1,000 ft of climbing, then +25% for stops. Not for navigation; carry the official park map.
