How this was made: elevation from AWS Terrain Tiles (USGS-derived, ~7 m cells at the detail scale), contoured every 20 ft on detail maps and 40 ft on the overview. Lake-loop trails are placed by hand against the terrain, accurate to roughly 50–150 m. Dashed trails are approximate corridors (±300 m): the Ice Age Trail pieces, Roznos Meadow, Steinke Basin, Parfrey's Glen and Gibraltar Rock. Parking spots for those are approximate too; use the park and ferry signage. Lengths of the dashed trails were adjusted to match published figures (Parfrey's Glen ~0.7 mi each way; Gibraltar Rock loop ~1.5 mi). Distances and climbing are computed from the drawn lines, so expect ±10–15%. Time uses your pace: 3 mph on flat ground plus 1 hour per 1,000 ft of climbing, then +25% for stops. Not for navigation.
