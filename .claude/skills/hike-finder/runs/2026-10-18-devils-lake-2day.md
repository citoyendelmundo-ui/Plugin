## Options

### 1. Recommended: Climb day → distance day (one new demand per day)
- **Day 1 (4–5 h): earned views, push = steep rock-stair gain with poles.**
  - Perimeter loop over both bluffs (~4.8 mi, ~1,000 ft) + the Grottos out-and-back (flat, ~1.6 mi).
  - ~6.5 mi, ~1,100 ft.
  - Clockwise vs counter-clockwise: put the gentler descent (East Bluff Woods side) last.
  - Time a bluff top for golden hour (~5–5:45 pm), then descend on the maintained trail before ~6:05.
- **Day 2 (5–7 h), gated by a morning check: push = duration on rolling terrain.**
  - Ice Age Trail uplands: Roznos Meadow + Steinke Basin + East Bluff uplands. ~8–11 mi, moderate gain.
  - Optional East Bluff finish if clean. Low stair load.
- **Why:** each day adds one new demand.
  - Day 1 doubles the steep gain *with poles*, which is also the poles test.
  - Day 2 extends hours where the hip load is lowest.
  - Back-to-back is unavoidable; Day 2's terrain choice manages it.

### 2. Max climbing both days (aggressive; stacks gain + stairs + back-to-back)
- **Day 1 (5–6 h):** perimeter loop + East Bluff Woods/uplands extension. ~8–9 mi, ~1,400–1,600 ft.
- **Day 2 (4–5 h):** perimeter loop reversed + Tumbled Rocks/Grottos. ~6–7 mi, ~1,000–1,100 ft.
- **Only if:** Day 1's first bluff with poles stays ≤2/10 and clears fast, and the Day 2 morning check is clean.

### 3. Long and gentle (if the Oct 1 check wasn't clean, poles aren't with you, or it rains)
- **Day 1 (4–5 h):** East Bluff + Tumbled Rocks loop (4.3 mi, 551 ft — the same gain as the 3 Dune Challenge) + Grottos + Roznos Meadow flat section.
- **Day 2 (5–7 h):** Ice Age Trail uplands.
- **Push = duration only.**

## In-day gates (all options)
- **Poles on every climb and every descent.**
- **Checkpoints:** at each bluff top and at each return to lake level, rate TFL, joint, and IT band 0–10.
- **Stop rule:** existing rule (4/10 = stop the steep work for the day). The proposed refinement (4/10 that clears in ≤1 min → pause, then continue only on non-steep terrain) is **not approved**.
- **Day 2 morning check:** use the Oct 1 protocol with a baseline anchor.
  - Clean → plan as designed.
  - Mild → Day 2 = flat/rolling only.
  - Hold → Tumbled Rocks/Grottos only, or rest.
- **Rain or wet rock:** no bluff stairs (wet quartzite + steep = stacked). Switch to Option 3 terrain.
- **No scrambles:** CCC, Balanced Rock, Potholes (hands-required = separate untested dimension).
