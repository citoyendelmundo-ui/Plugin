## Rain changes the map
- **Winners:**
  - sand soils (dunes firm up, drain fast);
  - forest canopy;
  - seasonal waterfalls (Illinois River canyons run after rain);
  - crowd-magnet parks, which empty out.
- **Losers:**
  - wet quartzite and sandstone stairs and ledges;
  - clay and floodplain trails (mud veto);
  - stream-crossing canyon floors;
  - fully open terrain with no shelter;
  - distant earned views (fog).

## Portfolio

### Rare-condition window — Starved Rock, eastern canyons, after rain (target: Fri Oct 2, late morning onward)
- **Route:**
  - From the Visitor Center via the French Canyon stairway: French Canyon → Wildcat Canyon, with overlooks at Lovers Leap, Eagle Cliff, and Beehive.
  - Optionally extend east to Ottawa and Kaskaskia canyons (Route 71 lots), or add St. Louis Canyon (Route 178 lot) as a separate short hike to its waterfall.
  - ~5–6 mi total.
  - **LaSalle and Tonti canyons are closed** (construction, including the Parkman's Plain lot).
- **Travel:** car ~1 h 45 min.
- **Readiness:** **Conditional.**
  - Body check first. **Wet stairs and sandstone are the single progression dimension** (wet footing).
  - Keep to maintained trails, stairs, and boardwalks. Look at the falls from the canyon mouths; **don't scramble canyon floors or cross high water**.
  - **Hold** if Friday still has thunder.
  - Poles Recommended (wet stairs plus hip flexion).
- **Value:** **Strong→Exceptional (provisional).** Drivers:
  - running waterfalls (water +1, rare timing +1);
  - weekday plus post-rain crowd suppression (lifts the TASTE-009 veto);
  - modest earned overlooks (+½).
  Mud exposure on unpaved segments is −½ to −1.
- **Confidence:** operational Medium (trail status verified by the IDNR notice, not today's daily status); fit Medium.
- **Why not:** muddy connectors; construction detours. Check the Visitor Center status the morning of.

### Best overall in light rain — Saugatuck Dunes SP (MI): South Trail + beach
- **Route:** South Trail loop (~5.2 mi, ~480 ft) through forested dunes to the beach, then back. Mostly under canopy, with a short beach section.
- **Travel:** car ~2 h 15 min (Eastern Time).
- **Readiness:** **Conditional (body check; sand progression as before).** Rain firms the sand, so there's no added footing hazard. **No-go on the beach and dune crests if there's thunder.** On the beach, the shell is required when it's below ~55°F with an onshore wind.
- **Value:** **Strong (provisional).** Drivers:
  - remote feel (TASTE-001);
  - lake (TASTE-004);
  - forest shelter;
  - empty trails.
  Views are fogged (+½ instead of +1).
- **Target:** Sun Oct 4 (early sprinkles), or any showery weekday.

### Best progression in light rain — Indiana Dunes SP: Trail 9 (+ 3 Dune Challenge if no thunder)
- **Route:** Trail 9 (~3.75 mi), forested dune ridges and blowouts, then optionally Trail 8 (the 3 Dune Challenge). ~5–7 mi.
- **Travel:** car ~1 h 10 min. Transit via South Shore Line to Dune Park plus a ~1.5 mi walk (less fun in rain).
- **Readiness:** **Conditional (body check).** The progression dimension stays **steep loose-sand climbs**. Wet sand gives *better* traction, so rain doesn't stack a second dimension here. **No-go on the dune summits with any thunder.**
- **Value:** **Strong (provisional).** Drivers: lake, earned summit view (+½ in fog), progression. Rain removes the weekend crowd veto.
- **Avoid:** **Trail 2 is closed** and floods in wet weather.

### Best forest-in-rain (Fells feel) — Southern Kettle Moraine, Ice Age Trail (smoother segments)
- **Route:** ~5–6 mi under forest canopy on gravelly glacial soils, which drain well. Choose segments with fewer exposed roots and rocks.
- **Travel:** car ~1 h 55 min.
- **Readiness:** **Conditional.** Wet roots and rocks are the single progression dimension. Keep distance ≤6 mi and gain modest. Road crossings give frequent bailouts.
- **Value:** **Strong (provisional).** Drivers: forest and kettle lakes (TASTE-004, Fells anchor), flow (TASTE-003), rain shelter. Peak color arrives around the third week of October.
- **Why not:** archery season is active, so wear something bright.

### Low-friction backup — Kankakee River SP: Rock Creek canyon + river trail
- **Route:** Rock Creek canyon waterfall (runs after rain) plus the river bluff trail, ~3–5 mi.
- **Travel:** car ~1 h 15 min.
- **Readiness:** Actionable on the main trails. The limestone canyon edges are slick, so stay on the trail. Don't enter the creek.
- **Value:** **Worthwhile→Strong (provisional).** Water and waterfall timing; shorter and less remote. Confidence: operational Low (not researched in depth this run).

## Excluded or downgraded in rain
- **Devil's Lake (bluffs, stone stairs, talus):** wet quartzite on sustained steep ascent **stacks two progression dimensions**. Excluded on rain days.
  - **If your committed color weekday turns rainy, move the date.** Drizzle-only fallback: the East Bluff Woods dirt approach, no stone-step descents, no West Bluff. Tumbled Rocks is also slick.
- **Matthiessen Lower Dells during or right after rain:** stream crossings and mud. Upper Dells rim viewing is acceptable 24+ h after rain, when the falls are running and the crossings have dropped.
- **Indiana Dunes Trail 2 and Cowles Bog:** closed and flood-prone (Trail 2); bog mud (Cowles).
- **Mississippi Palisades:** steep dirt bluff trails get slick in rain; save for dry days.
- **Illinois Beach SP:** drains well but has no canopy, so wind-driven rain on open dune-and-swale makes it tolerable, not fun. Dry days only.
- **Thunderstorm days** (e.g., Oct 1): Hold for all exposed terrain.
