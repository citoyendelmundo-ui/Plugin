# Hike Finder run — Chicagoland · 2026-09-25

- **Origin:** Bryn Mawr Red Line (Edgewater).
- **Assumptions:** car available; body state Normal (confirm before any progression outing); solo or companion.
- **Taste profile:** v0.2 (calibration session 1 + follow-up).
- **Verification:** conditions and closures checked by web search on 2026-09-25 (sources at bottom). **Recheck official status pages the morning of.**
- **Drive and transit times** are estimates, not routed.
- **Scan coverage:** partial. Lakeshore dunes, Baraboo Hills, Kettle Moraine, Illinois River valley, and the Mississippi bluffs were scanned. The Driftless interior (Wyalusing, Wildcat Mountain) and southwest Michigan beyond Saugatuck were not, so absence there isn't a finding.

## Season-wide facts that shape both runs
- **Swimming lane is off.** Lake Michigan lifeguard season ended at Labor Day, so any swim is unguarded open water, which is Inventory-only. The hike+swim role is inactive until summer.
- **Bugs** are fading. Bog and wet woods can still hold mosquitoes into early October. Leaf-off (November) removes the bug veto almost entirely.
- **Hunting:**
  - Indiana firearm deer season Nov 14–29.
  - Illinois firearm Nov 20–22 (and Dec 3–6).
  - Wisconsin gun Nov 21–29.
  - Archery runs from ~Oct 1 in all three states.
  - Indiana state parks also run November deer-reduction hunts that *close* parks on set days. Verify dates before any Indiana state park visit after Nov 1.
  - Michigan's firearm opener is traditionally Nov 15 (not verified here).
  - Wear blaze orange anywhere hunting is allowed from mid-November.
- **Fall color:**
  - Baraboo Hills/Devil's Lake: best during the first three weeks of October. The statewide peak outlook is around Oct 12.
  - Southern Wisconsin/Kettle Moraine: typically the third week of October.
  - Chicago-area lakeshore: later (late Oct).

## RUN A — This weekend (Sat Sep 26 – Sun Sep 27)

**Conditions:**
- Mild: Sat high ~67°F, low ~56°F; Sun high ~71°F, low ~57°F. Precipitation not confirmed; check the NWS the night before.
- No heat or bug veto expected on open dune terrain.
- Weekend crowding is the dominant taste risk.

### Best overall — Saugatuck Dunes State Park (MI): South Trail + beach + North Trail
- **Route:** South Trail loop (~5.2 mi, ~480 ft cumulative gain per AllTrails) to the beach. Add the North Trail (~1.9 mi) for ~7–8 mi total, which is within the demonstrated 10 mi. Rolling sand, forested dunes, 2.5 mi of undeveloped Lake Michigan shore.
- **Travel:** car ~2 h 15 min, and Michigan is on Eastern Time (lose an hour on the clock). No practical transit.
- **Readiness:** **Conditional → Actionable after a quick pre-hike body check.** Loose sand on climbs is Untested (USR-HIK-015), so this is a controlled progression. The single progression dimension is **sand footing**; distance, gain, and markings stay familiar. Bring poles for sandy climbs.
- **Value:** **Exceptional (provisional).** Drivers:
  - openness and remote feel (TASTE-001): the park is known for leaving crowds behind;
  - Lake Michigan (TASTE-004);
  - dune-crest views (TASTE-006);
  - sand progression (TASTE-002).
  Crowd veto is unlikely.
- **Confidence:** operational Medium; fit Medium.
- **Why not:** longest drive of this weekend's options; loose-sand fatigue is unknown. **Turnaround trigger:** hip discomfort during sandy climbs, or a pace drop of more than 25%. **Bailout:** the Beach Trail (0.75 mi) is the short exit back to the lot.
- **Safety:** unguarded water, so no swimming. Keep back from dune edges, which can drop off suddenly. Companion optional.

### Best progression — Indiana Dunes State Park: Trail 9 + 3 Dune Challenge (Trail 8) + beach
- **Route:** Trail 9 (~3.75 mi) along the dune ridge and through blowouts, with lake views. Then the 3 Dune Challenge (1.5 mi over Mt. Jackson, Mt. Holden, Mt. Tom; ~550 ft of steep sand climbing in short pitches). Finish with a beach walk. About 6–7 mi total.
- **Travel:** car ~1 h 10 min. Transit ~2 h 15 min+ (Red Line → Millennium Station → South Shore Line to Dune Park, then ~1.5 mi walk; check the weekend schedule).
- **Readiness:** **Conditional: needs a fresh body check.**
  - The progression dimension is **short, steep loose-sand climbs**. That touches two Untested rows (sand and steep ascent), but the dose is short, and the 3 Dune Challenge starts and ends near the lot, so bailout is trivial.
  - Do Trail 9 first as the warm-up and read your hip response before committing to the Challenge.
  - Poles are **Recommended** (repeated uphill hip flexion).
- **Value:** **Strong (provisional).** Drivers:
  - lake plus dune-top views (TASTE-004, -006);
  - clear, legible progression (TASTE-002).
  Crowd veto −1 on a warm weekend, which you can offset by being at the trailhead by 8:00.
- **Confidence:** operational Medium (state park trail status not confirmed online; the park-specific search found no closures); fit Medium.
- **Log:** symptom check pre-hike, at each dune summit, post-hike, that evening, and the next day. It's the first dose of sand-plus-steep, so this log is calibration evidence.

### Best low-friction — Illinois Beach State Park (South Unit): Dead River + Dunes loop + shoreline
- **Route:** Dead River and Dunes loop (~2.5 mi, ~26 ft gain, gravel/sand), extended with a shoreline walk to ~5–6 mi. Rare dune-and-swale terrain, black-oak savanna, and open lake horizon.
- **Travel:** car ~55 min. Transit ~1 h 45 min–2 h 15 min: Metra UP-North to Zion (weekend trains are infrequent; check the schedule), then ~2 mi walk or bike, or Divvy/bike-on-train.
- **Readiness:** **Actionable (Routine).** Flat, maintained, within the demonstrated envelope; no fresh check needed.
- **Value:** **Strong (provisional).** Drivers:
  - openness (TASTE-001, moderate: open lake horizon, but not remote);
  - water (TASTE-004).
  No progression and no elevated views.
- **Confidence:** operational Medium; fit Medium.
- **Why not:** it's the weakest on views. Choose it for a car-free day or a short outing.

### Stretch pick — Devil's Lake SP (WI): East Bluff
- **Travel:** ~3 h each way. Early color only this weekend; peak arrives in the first three weeks of October.
- **Verdict:** hold it for the Run B rare window, not this weekend.

### Excluded this weekend
- **Starved Rock:** the $18M trail overhaul is staggered, so some trails are still closed. Weekend crowds and bottlenecks (veto), and canyon waterfalls are usually dry in fall.
- **Cowles Bog (Indiana Dunes NP):** bog-adjacent mosquitoes are still plausible (veto). Revisit leaf-off in November.
- **Glenwood Dunes and Little Calumet/Mnoké Prairie trails:** partially **closed** through Dec 31 (Marquette Greenway construction). Bailly/Chellberg is partially closed.
- **Warren Dunes (MI):** bigger crowds than Saugatuck for similar drivers.
- **Waterfall Glen, Palos/Swallow Cliff:** road/traffic noise (veto).

### Rare-condition window and season's best overall — Devil's Lake SP: East Bluff + Devil's Doorway + West Bluff
- **Route:**
  - East Bluff Trail via the East Bluff Woods approach to Devil's Doorway and the bluff-top overlooks.
  - Descend to the north shore, then climb the West Bluff Trail (stone steps).
  - ~5–6 mi, roughly 1,000–1,200 ft cumulative gain (estimate; verify).
  - Quartzite bluffs 500 ft above a glacial lake, CCC-era stonework, and Ice Age Trail segments.
- **Travel:** car ~3 h, within the cap. Allow ~6–7 h for the hike with dwell stops. No practical transit.
- **Readiness:** **Conditional: needs a fresh body check plus a progression rationale.**
  - Gain exceeds the provisional 800 ft routine, and the stair climbs are sustained ascent (USR-HIK-013). **That is the single progression dimension.** Keep distance ≤6 mi and stay on maintained trail.
  - Poles Recommended.
  - **Do not** stack in the CCC Trail, Balanced Rock, or Potholes scrambles; those are hands-required, Controlled progression with a companion Recommended.
  - Stay back from overlook edges.
  - Bailout: split the day. Do East Bluff only and skip the West Bluff climb if the hip is talking.
- **Value:** **Exceptional (provisional).** The closest Midwest analog to Claremont. Drivers:
  - views from elevated vantage (TASTE-006);
  - lake below (TASTE-004);
  - peak-color rare window;
  - progression (TASTE-002);
  - history: CCC stonework, effigy mounds nearby (TASTE-013).
  Weekend crowding at peak is severe (veto −1 → Strong). **Go on a weekday, or be at the trailhead by 7:30.**
- **Confidence:** operational Medium (no 2026 closure found; the DNR posts notices when active); fit Medium-High.
- **Watch:** Thursday color reports on DevilsLakeWisconsin.com and the Travel Wisconsin Fall Color Report. Commit when the Baraboo area reads 60–80% color.

### Best progression — Southern Kettle Moraine, Ice Age Trail (e.g., Scuppernong / Emma Carlin segments)
- **Route:** ~8 mi point-to-point or out-and-back on glacial kames and moraines. Rolling, with roots and rocks. Gain around ~800 ft (estimate; verify per segment).
- **Travel:** car ~1 h 55 min.
- **Readiness:** **Conditional: needs a body check.** The progression dimension is **continuous uneven footing** (USR-HIK-012). Distance stays inside the demonstrated 10 mi and gain stays near the routine limit, so one dimension moves. Road crossings give frequent bailout points.
- **Value:** **Strong (provisional).** Drivers:
  - progression (TASTE-002);
  - flow terrain with kettle-lake payoffs (TASTE-003, -004 partial);
  - peak color around the third week of October.
  Lower openness and views than Devil's Lake.
- **Why not:** archery hunting is active and state forest hunting is allowed, so wear something bright. Gun season opens Nov 21, after the window.

### Best restorative/immersive (Fells-feel) — Matthiessen State Park: Dells + River Area
- **Route:** Upper and Lower Dells canyon loop plus the River Area bluff trail, ~5–6 mi. Sandstone canyon, forest, and the Vermilion River. The quiet sibling of Starved Rock.
- **Travel:** car ~1 h 45 min.
- **Readiness:** **Actionable (Routine)** on the main loop. The canyon floor can be wet or muddy after rain, which touches the mud veto and wet footing (Controlled progression). Choose a dry-spell day, or stay on rim trails.
- **Value:** **Strong (provisional).** Drivers:
  - water and forest (TASTE-004; the Fells anchor, TASTE-012);
  - moderate crowding (much lighter than Starved Rock).
- **Best window:** late October color, on a dry weekday.

### Views alternative — Mississippi Palisades SP (IL)
- **Travel:** ~2 h 45 min.
- **What it offers:** bluff-top overlooks of the Mississippi, with late-October color. Openness, views, and water on big-river scale, with far fewer people than Devil's Lake.
- **Readiness:** Routine to Controlled progression depending on route (some steep bluff trails). Research the route before committing.
- **Value:** **Strong→Exceptional (provisional).** Confidence: operational Low (not researched in depth this run); fit Medium.

### Best low-friction and leaf-off rare window — lakeshore in early November
- **Where:** Illinois Beach SP (Metra-reachable) or Indiana Dunes on a **north/northeast gale day**: big surf, open sky, no bugs, empty trails.
- **Readiness:** Actionable (Routine) on the trails. **Stay off piers and breakwaters and keep back from the wave-run-up zone.** Waves plus cold is a No-go for anything near the water's edge.
- **Value:** **Strong→Exceptional (provisional)**, as a rare-condition window combining openness and water.
- **Timing:** Indiana sites before Nov 14. Verify any Indiana state park deer-reduction closure dates.

### Excluded or deferred this season
- **Starved Rock:** construction closures continue through 2026, and there's peak-weekend crowding. Weekday-only, and only after checking the Visitor Center status.
- **Dolly Sods:** outside the envelope (~10 h drive). It is the single best future test of TASTE-001 on a dedicated trip (flag for spring/summer 2027 planning).
- **Hike+swim:** off until summer; swimming is unguarded-only now.

## ADDENDUM (2026-09-25, later): re-ranked after new taste inputs
New records:
- **Weekday available** and **inclined to overnight near a great hike** (TASTE-014).
- **Claremont = earning the view with the climb** (TASTE-006 revised). Earned views now get +1; views without a climb get +½.

### What changed in the ranking
1. **Devil's Lake is now the clear season headliner, on a weekday, as an overnight.** Its bluff stairs *are* the approach to the view, so the earned view is the core of the route. Value: **Exceptional**. The weekday removes the crowd veto.
2. **Mississippi Palisades drops a notch** (Strong). Its best overlooks can be reached by car, so the views are less earned.
3. **Indiana Dunes' 3 Dune Challenge rises.** The Mt. Tom summit view is earned by steep sand climbing.
4. **Saugatuck + Hoffmaster becomes a Michigan overnight option** (details below).

### Devil's Lake overnight, recommended structure (weekday, Oct 5–18; commit off Thursday color reports)
- **Day 1 (afternoon arrival, ~3 h drive):**
  - East Bluff via East Bluff Woods, then Devil's Doorway and the east bluff-top overlooks.
  - Time the bluff top for **late-afternoon or sunset light**. Headlamp owned; descend on the maintained trail before full dark.
  - Earned view; ~3 mi; roughly half the park's total climbing.
- **Overnight:** Devil's Lake SP campground (open through October; showers until about mid-month) or Baraboo lodging. Feasibility only; not sourced in detail.
- **Day 2 (morning), gated on a next-morning symptom check:**
  - **Clean** → West Bluff Trail up the stone steps to the west bluff overlooks, early, when the lake is still and the trail empty. Earned view #2.
  - **Hip talking** → Tumbled Rocks Trail: flat, along the lake at the base of the West Bluff, with talus and lake views. Its pavement is rough, so watch footing.
- **Why split beats one big day:**
  - Each day's gain stays near the routine envelope (~800 ft) instead of 1,000–1,200 ft in one push.
  - The single progression dimension becomes **back-to-back days of climbing**, which is Untested, and it has a built-in Day 2 downgrade.
  - You get two light windows (sunset and early morning) at the park's best viewpoints, with no crowds.
- **Readiness:** Conditional (fresh body check Day 1; symptom check Day 2). No scrambles (CCC, Balanced Rock, Potholes). Poles Recommended.
- **Value:** Exceptional (provisional). Drivers: earned views (TASTE-006), lake (TASTE-004), peak-color rare window, progression (TASTE-002), CCC history (TASTE-013). No crowd veto on a weekday.
- **Confidence:** operational Medium; fit **High** (all three main drivers plus Claremont-shaped).
- **Log:** full symptom phases for both days, plus next-day recovery after Day 2. This is valuable multi-day calibration evidence.

### Michigan overnight option: Saugatuck Dunes + P.J. Hoffmaster (early-mid October weekday, or before the Nov 15 firearm opener)
- **Day 1:** Saugatuck Dunes South + North trails + beach (~7–8 mi, loose sand; progression dimension = sand).
- **Day 2** (~40 min north), gated on symptom check: Hoffmaster Dune Climb Stairway (~220 steps to a Lake Michigan panorama; an earned view in a short dose), plus the forested dune trails and beach.
- **Access flag:** the Dune Overlook Trail and stairway were **closed for construction in summer 2025**, and 2026 status wasn't verified. Treat it as **Unknown/Hold on access** until confirmed on the Michigan DNR park page.
- **Value:** Strong→Exceptional (openness + water + earned view). Confidence: operational Low-Medium (Hoffmaster access unverified); fit Medium-High.
